Category Wise Refund Rules

1. Electrical Work & Products

30 days workmanship warranty on electrical work. For manufacturing defects in materials, warranty will be provided from the brand's service center. Wires, switches once opened or used cannot be returned. If faulty wiring is due to client side, it will be chargeable.

✔ 30 Days Workmanship Warranty
2. Stationery & General Items

Return only in seal-packed, unused condition. Used items like registers, pens, files, dusters cannot be returned. If product is defective from company (e.g. pen ink not working) you must inform within 24 hours. In bulk orders (500+ qty) 2-3% damage is allowed as per company policy.

✔ 24 Hour Intimation Must
3. Printing & Branding Work

After design approval on WhatsApp/Email there is 0% refund / cancellation. If spelling, number, logo was approved by client, responsibility will be client's. If printing is wrong from our side (color mistake, cutting mistake) we will do a full free reprint. 10-15% color difference in paper quality is normal in printing.

⚠ No Refund After Approval
4. Computer, Laptop & IT Products

New laptops/PCs will have brand warranty. IES will only help with replacement. Refurbished products have 30-90 days IES testing warranty. Physical damage, water damage, password lock voids warranty. We are not responsible for software or data loss.

✔ Brand + IES Double Support
